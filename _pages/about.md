I am an Applied Scientist at Sparxity (formerly SpiNNcloud Systems), working on architecture retrofitting and post-training of large language models. Previously, I was a Postdoc at King's College London. I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Manchester, supervised by Prof. Steve Furber.

My research interests include **post-training** with reinforcement learning, **self-improvement**, and **decoding methods** for large language models.
